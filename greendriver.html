What it does

Mileage
GPS logs the route while the screen is off and while you deliver on foot. Auto-Drive notices you have started moving and offers to open a trip, so a forgotten tap is not a lost deduction.
Corrected distance
Raw GPS reads long. Each vehicle learns its own correction factor from the odometer readings you type in — on the test car it holds within ±1.5% of the dash.
OBD-II
Optional $20 Bluetooth dongle reads speed straight off the engine bus, which is what the odometer itself counts. Used to calibrate GPS rather than replace it.
Real pay
Take-home $/hour after fuel and vehicle cost, broken out per platform, so you can see which one actually paid better this week. Not the advertised block rate.
Amazon Flex caps
40 h per rolling 7 days, 8 h a day — 50 and 10 in peak. Counts blocks you have accepted but not yet driven, and shows which upcoming days are already full.
Expenses
Fuel, maintenance, insurance, tolls, parking. Receipt scanning reads the amount, vendor and date on the phone — no image is ever uploaded.
Which method wins
Standard mileage against actual expenses, side by side, including MACRS vehicle depreciation — the piece most trackers leave out, and the one that can reverse the answer on a car you own.
Tax paperwork
Self-employment tax estimate, quarterly summaries, a PDF report, CSV, and an audit-readiness check against what IRS Pub. 463 actually requires — flagged trip by trip.
Your data
Stays on the phone. No account, no server, no ads, no advertising ID. Two-generation backup to a folder you pick.
Languages
English, Español, Português, Русский, Türkçe, O‘zbekcha, Українська, العربية (right-to-left supported).

What it is not

  • Not connected to any platform. It does not log in to Amazon Flex, DoorDash or anyone else, and cannot read your account. It records your own driving.
  • Not tax advice. The deduction and depreciation figures are estimates to hand a preparer. Anything resting on a tax year outside the published tables is flagged for checking rather than guessed.
  • Not a route optimiser, and not a job finder.
  • Not reliable with battery optimisation on. Android will kill background tracking. The app walks you through turning it off — it is the one setup step that matters.
