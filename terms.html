Mileage is estimated from your phone's GPS and, where you provide them, your own odometer entries and an optional OBD-II adapter. GPS accuracy varies by device, signal and environment, and background tracking can be stopped by your phone's battery settings. Compass headings can be wrong near magnets, steel or a charging cable; the app warns when it detects this, but cannot detect every case.

We make no guarantee that recorded mileage, timing, headings or costs are error-free. Review your data, especially before relying on it for a tax filing.
