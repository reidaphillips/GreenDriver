What it does

The dial
Turns with the world while the needle holds north, the way a real card compass does. The amber index at twelve o'clock reads your heading. Every letter is upright when you face it.
True north
Corrected by the local declination — 14° in Spokane, over 20° in parts of Alaska. Ignoring it there will walk you off a ridge.
Eight faces
Midnight, Carbon, Blueprint, Brass, Crimson, Forest, Rose Gold and Arctic. The picker shows live dials, not colour swatches.
Waypoints
Mark where you parked. A marker on the rim points back to it with the distance, updating as you walk.
Sun and moon
Bearing and height for both, drawn on the dial — hollow when below the horizon — with the moon's phase and how much of it is lit.
Position
Coordinates in decimal or degrees/minutes/seconds, altitude in feet, speed in mph, and the GPS accuracy next to them. Share your position to any app.
Honesty check
Measured field strength and dip angle against what the Earth's own model says they should be where you stand. Beside a desk speaker the test phone read 42° of dip where the truth is 68° — and the heading was 75° wrong.
The right advice
A magnet stuck to your phone and a speaker on your desk need opposite fixes. The app tells them apart: wave it in a figure 8, or move away.
Offline
Declination, sun, moon, bearings and distances are all computed on the device. No internet permission at all — it works at the bottom of a canyon.
History
Headings you actually held, with the time and for how long. Not every twitch on the way round.

What it is not

  • Not a map. It is an instrument — bearings, not navigation.
  • It cannot correct interference, only detect it. A magnet fixed to the phone is absorbed by calibration automatically. A speaker on the desk stays put while the phone moves, which leaves one measurement and two unknowns — not solvable, only escapable. The app says which one you have.
  • Location is optional and never asked for at launch. The needle works without it; coordinates, true north, waypoints and the sky do not.
