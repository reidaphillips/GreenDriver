Why these exist

The delivery platforms do not report your deductible mileage back to you. They report what they paid. The gap between those two numbers is the whole reason the Mileage Tracker exists — at 76¢ a mile it is usually worth more than a day's pay.

Both apps keep your data on your phone. No sign-in, no server, no advertising ID, and no subscription wall in front of features that already work.

Not affiliated with Amazon, Uber, DoorDash, Instacart or any other platform. These apps do not log in to those services or read anything from them — they record your own driving with your own phone.
